## Use cases

### Search occupancy (query)

`unit/search-occupancy.handler.spec.ts`, `unit/search-occupancy.controller.spec.ts`

- **Search occupancy**
  - shows the manager of a practice every calendar of it, and nothing of another practice
  - shows a practitioner their own calendars only
  - refuses the occupancy of a practice to the manager of another one
  - keeps the days and the calendar asked for, in the order asked
  - never widens what a practitioner sees, whatever calendar is asked for
- **GET /practices/:practiceId/occupancy**
  - answers each line without the practice, which the path already names
  - asks for the practice of the path, with the calendar, the range of days, the order and the page of the query
  - refuses %o, which the occupancy cannot be read by, with 422

## Model

### Measured outcome (value object)

`unit/measured-outcome.vo.spec.ts`

- **MeasuredOutcome**
  - is equal to another outcome in the same state over the same time
  - covers its time to the nearest minute
  - tells whether it stands in one of the states asked for
  - covers no time when it ends as it starts, and refuses an end before its start
  - keeps its own copy of the instants it was given

### Occupancy figures (value object)

`unit/occupancy-figures.vo.spec.ts`

- **OccupancyFigures**
  - is equal to other figures with the same counts and minutes
  - reads the time held and the appointments that hold it
  - refuses a figure below zero or not whole
  - refuses fewer appointments held than those completed and those missed
  - gives its figures as the line of a read model takes them

### Occupancy measure (domain service)

`unit/occupancy-measure.service.spec.ts`

- **OccupancyMeasureService**
  - holds no state of its own
  - measures an empty day as nothing held and nothing counted
  - holds the time of the appointments held, completed and missed by the patient
  - counts the time of each appointment to the nearest minute
  - frees the time of a cancelled or expired appointment, and counts the cancellations by party

### Outcome period inverted (error)

### occupancy-reader (contract), in integration

`integration/occupancy-reader.contract.spec.ts`

- **The occupancy measured by %s**
  - counts the appointments of a calendar on a day, state by state, and the time of those that hold it
  - counts the time of each appointment to the nearest minute
  - keeps a line for a day whose only appointment was cancelled, with nothing held
  - counts a rescheduled appointment on its new day only, and has no line for the day it left empty
  - no longer counts an outcome that was removed
  - answers nothing where no appointment was ever announced
- **The occupancy read from %s**
  - lists the lines of one practice, never those of another, by day then by calendar
  - keeps the days of a range, both ends included, latest first when asked
  - keeps the calendars asked for
  - orders the lines by the time held, the key deciding between two that hold as much
  - refuses to be filtered by a figure
- **The occupancy an actor may read from %s**
  - is narrowed to the calendars the actor measures, total included
  - is not widened by asking for a calendar the actor does not measure
  - is empty for an actor who measures no calendar
- **The pages of the occupancy read from %s**
  - are slices of the same order, each with the total of the lines
  - still say the total past the last line
  - follow one another by cursor, each line once, in the order of the time held
