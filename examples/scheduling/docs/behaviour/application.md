### journey-authorization (end to end), in e2e

`e2e/journey-authorization.e2e.spec.ts`

- **The desk of a practitioner, delegated to an assistant**
  - refuses the assistant who books for a patient before the delegation, and lets them once it is granted
  - keeps the opening hours from the delegate unless the delegation includes the availability
  - takes the desk back from the assistant when the delegation is revoked
- **What one practice and one patient hold, kept from the others**
  - refuses the agenda and the occupancy of a practice to the manager of another, and lists them none of its appointments
  - refuses a patient the appointment of another, and shows them its slot as busy without the patient
- **An appointment that starts in less than a day**
  - is no longer the patient's to cancel or to move: the notice they owe the practice has run out
  - is still the practitioner's to cancel, who must say why
- **Support staff acting through a patient**
  - read what the patient reads and are refused every write
  - leave their name in the audit trail, on what they were allowed and on what they were refused

### journey-booking (end to end), in e2e

`e2e/journey-booking.e2e.spec.ts`

- **A patient books, is told, and cancels: the four contexts follow**
  - gives the slot to the patient who books first and answers SLOT_TAKEN to the second
  - lists the appointment to its patient, filtered and sorted, and not to another patient
  - tells the patient who booked, and nobody else
  - shows the minutes the practice holds to its manager
  - lets the patient cancel a week ahead: the list, the occupancy and the slot follow

### journey-clearance (end to end), in e2e

`e2e/journey-clearance.e2e.spec.ts`

- **A practitioner goes away: a worker clears the calendar, then the time off is accepted**
  - cancels the appointments of the period one by one, says how far it went, and the calendar can close
  - shows a clearance to whoever may clear the calendar, and to nobody else

### journey-documents (end to end), in e2e

`e2e/journey-documents.e2e.spec.ts`

- **A document attached to an appointment: sent as it streams, fetched through a short link, removed**
  - keeps the document of the patient, serves it through a link that names it, and forgets it once removed
  - refuses a file whose bytes are not what it declares, and keeps the document from another patient

### journey-observability (end to end), in e2e

`e2e/journey-observability.e2e.spec.ts`

- **One booking, followed from outside: its trace, its log lines, its error report**
  - is one trace, from the request to what notification and analytics do of the fact
  - writes the log lines of the request and of its consumer under one correlation id and one trace id
  - reports an unexpected failure with the trace id of its request, and tells the client nothing of it

### journey-tenancy (end to end), in e2e

`e2e/journey-tenancy.e2e.spec.ts`

- **Two networks of practices served by one application**
  - keeps the data of one network out of the reach of the other: its calendars are not found there
  - lists to the people of a network the appointments of that network only
  - refuses at the edge a request that names a network its caller does not belong to
  - holds a person to one network: the subject known in one is refused in the other, and kept where it was
  - finds nothing of a network the deployment does not serve, even for a token that names it
- **Platform staff, who belong to no network**
  - read the trail of one network through an elevation the trail recorded first
  - read nothing of a network when the trail is searched for another one
