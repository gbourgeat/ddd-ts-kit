### get-opening-hours (end to end), in e2e

`e2e/get-opening-hours.e2e.spec.ts`

- **Get opening hours, end to end: what a cache may keep, and its purge after the change**
  - answers the hours to anybody, kept by every cache, filed under the calendar, and 304 to who holds them
  - keeps the day of a calendar in the browser of its caller only, asked again each time, 304 when nothing changed
  - purges the hours once the command that changes them committed, and nothing for a command refused
  - answers 412 to a change made on hours that changed since they were read, and makes it on the current version
  - purges the hours after any change of the calendar, so that the version they name is the one a change is made on
  - answers 404 to a calendar that does not exist, which no cache keeps
  - serves, once another change of the calendar committed, the version a change of the hours is then made on
  - serves the hours from Varnish until the command that changes them, then the new ones
- **Get opening hours, end to end: the copy of one network is never the answer of another**
  - names the network header in Vary, so that a shared cache keeps one copy per network
  - answers 404 to the calendar of another network, whoever asks there, which no cache keeps
