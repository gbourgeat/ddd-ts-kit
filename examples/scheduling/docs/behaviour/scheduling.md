### Appointment (aggregate), in calendars

`unit/calendars/appointment.aggregate.spec.ts`

- **Appointment**
  - records that it was booked, holding its slot with nothing left to confirm
  - records that it was booked as a request, with the deadline of its confirmation
  - holds its slot, buffer included, while it is requested or booked
  - holds nothing once it is each case
  - is still to come for its patient until it starts
- **Appointment, annotated by the staff**
  - records the internal note, and keeps it whatever its standing becomes
  - refuses a note on an appointment that has ended
- **Appointment, from confirmation to its end**
  - is booked once its request is confirmed, and has no deadline any more
  - refuses to confirm an appointment that is already booked
  - is cancelled by its patient without a reason
  - is cancelled by the practice with the reason it gave, even after its start
  - moves to another slot in one step, saying what it held before
  - announces the slot it left when it is rescheduled
  - is completed once it has started
  - refuses to be completed before its start
  - is marked as a no-show once it has started
  - refuses to be marked as a no-show before its start
- **Appointment, expiry and what follows an end**
  - expires at its deadline when nobody confirmed it
  - does not expire before its deadline
  - expires once: a second expiry of the same appointment changes nothing
  - does not expire once it is confirmed
  - does not change once it is each case
- *Generated by the kit*
  - **Appointment**
    - is born with its identity
    - is rehydrated in its stored state without recording a new fact
