### Reminder (aggregate)

`unit/reminder.aggregate.spec.ts`

- **Reminder**
  - is born planned, due 24 hours before the start of its appointment
  - is not due earlier than 24 hours before the start
  - is sent once it is due, and records the fact with its recipient, its appointment and the start
  - refuses to be sent before it is due
  - refuses to be sent twice for the same start
  - refuses to be sent once the appointment has started
  - refuses to be sent once withdrawn
  - changes nothing when planned again for the same start, even once sent
  - is planned again, and due again, when planned for the same start once withdrawn
  - is due again, 24 hours before the new start, when its appointment moves
  - is withdrawn while planned, and stays sent once sent
- *Generated by the kit*
  - **Reminder**
    - is born with its identity
    - is rehydrated in its stored state without recording a new fact
