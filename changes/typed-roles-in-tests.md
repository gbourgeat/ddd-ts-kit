**The actor of an end-to-end test holds roles of the role vocabulary of the platform, and a misspelled role no longer compiles.** Under `--authz rbac` and `--authz abac`, where `init` writes `Role` in `src/platform/auth/authorization/role.ts`, `test/e2e/support/create-credentials.ts` types the roles of a test actor with it (`readonly roles?: readonly Role[]`), whatever the authenticator (`none`, `jwt`, `keycloak`, `passport`): `application.as({ id: 'ada', roles: ['admn'] })` is refused by the compiler (`Type '"admn"' is not assignable to type 'Role'`) and the editor completes the role. `generate e2e` writes the role by its key, `roles: [Role.Admin]`, with its import: the administrator of the vocabulary, or its first role when it names none. Under relationships and a custom authorizer, which start without that vocabulary, the roles stay strings, as before.

`Actor.roles` of `@ddd-ts-kit/kernel` stays `readonly string[]`, and `RoleBasedAuthorizer` of `@ddd-ts-kit/auth` is not typed by the role names: a role may be declared by `inherits` alone, which a single type of the keys of `roles` refuses.

To move: in a project already generated under RBAC or ABAC, in `test/e2e/support/create-credentials.ts`, add `import type { Role } from '@/platform/auth/authorization/role';` and replace `readonly roles?: readonly string[];` of `TestActor` by `readonly roles?: readonly Role[];`. The compiler then lists each spec that names a role the vocabulary does not hold: write it `Role.<Key>`, or add the role to `role.ts`.
