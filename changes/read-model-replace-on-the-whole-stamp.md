**The conditional replace of a read model row compares the whole stamp on the four ORM, and Knex and TypeORM read the instant of a stamp back to the millisecond.** A write of a read model replaces the row only if it still holds the stamp that was read. The in-memory table and `@ddd-ts-kit/opensearch` compared the event id, the instant and the version; `@ddd-ts-kit/knex`, `@ddd-ts-kit/drizzle`, `@ddd-ts-kit/prisma` and `@ddd-ts-kit/typeorm` left the version out, so a row whose version alone had moved (two domain events of one name and one aggregate in the same millisecond share the two others) was replaced by a write computed from the row before it. The four now compare the version too, NULL matching NULL, and `sameStamp(left, right)` of `@ddd-ts-kit/kernel` is exported for a `ReadModelRows` of your own. On `@ddd-ts-kit/knex` and `@ddd-ts-kit/typeorm`, the instant of a stamp was read back through the text of the `Date` the driver gave, which has no milliseconds: the stamp read never matched the row, and the second event of a key ended in `ConcurrencyError` whenever the first carried milliseconds. It is read as the date it is. The order of stamps is unchanged, and now said where it is read (`isNewer`, `docs/concepts/cqrs.md`, "Which write for which event"): total among stamps that all carry a version and among stamps that carry none, decided by the instants between one of each, which is not an order, so a key should see one kind, the one mix that is meant being a deletion made outside the history of the key, such as an erasure. `readModelStoreContract` and `readModelRowsContract` of `@ddd-ts-kit/testing` hold the replace on the whole stamp, the stamp at the millisecond, the same row whatever the order of three deliveries of one kind, and such a deletion.

To move: a `ReadModelRows` of your own compares the version in `replace`, with `sameStamp`, and gives back the instant it was given, to the millisecond; run `readModelStoreContract` again on your stores. A workaround that rounded the instant of a stamp to the second for Knex or TypeORM can go.
