**`assertTopology` of `@ddd-ts-kit/kafka` fails with `BROKER_UNAVAILABLE` when the cluster cannot be reached, even with no topic to create.** It reached the cluster only to create a missing topic, so a service that subscribed to nothing yet, or whose topics all existed, or that may not create topics, started and logged `listening` with no broker. It now asks the cluster for its topics first, as the NATS, RabbitMQ and Redis buses reach their server: a generated service with `--broker kafka` fails at startup, never at its first record.
