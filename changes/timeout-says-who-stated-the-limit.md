**A call cut by the default time limit of the platform says so: `timeout(ms, { statedBy })`.** The platform of a generated project puts a default policy around every `external` or `gateway` adapter that states none: 2 seconds per attempt. An adapter that only hands its call to another adapter, slow by nature and bound with a longer limit of its own (a remote reader with 120 seconds), failed every call at 2 seconds, and the error named the outer adapter and nothing else. `timeout(ms, { statedBy })` of `@ddd-ts-kit/aspects`, and `@Timeout(ms, { statedBy })` of its decorators, now take who states the limit: the `TimeoutError` keeps it in `statedBy` and ends its message with it, its code `TIMEOUT` and its `timeoutMs` unchanged, and a `timeout` without `statedBy` writes the message it always wrote. The `default-policy.ts` that `init` writes names itself there, so the message reads `RemoteQuoteReader.read did not answer within 2000 ms (the default policy of the external and gateway adapters, OUTBOUND_TIMEOUT_MS: ownPolicy(timeout(<ms>)) on the declaration or the binding of this adapter states another, at least as long as the adapter it calls)`. See "A delegating adapter" of `docs/concepts/resilience.md`.

To move: a project generated earlier takes the message by adding `{ statedBy: '<who states it>' }` to the `timeout(settings.timeoutMs)` of its `src/platform/resilience/default-policy.ts`, as the template of this version does; nothing else changes, and a test that compares the whole message of a `TimeoutError` of the default policy reads the new ending.
