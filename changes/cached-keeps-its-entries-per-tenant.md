**The `cached` aspect and the `Cached` decorator keep their entries per tenant.** In work that names a tenant, an entry is filed under that tenant as `tenantCacheKey` does, and concurrent identical calls are shared within one tenant. `shared: true` keeps one entry for every tenant; work that names no tenant keeps the key as written; work under an elevation (`acrossTenants`) neither reads nor writes the cache and runs the call. `AspectRuntime` gains `scope`, the `ExecutionScope` `cached` reads the tenant from, and a generated composition root gives it to `installAspectRuntime`. See [Cache and coordination](docs/tools/cache-and-coordination.md).

To move: in `src/platform/composition/compose-application.ts`, or a composition root written by hand, add `scope` to `installAspectRuntime` (`scope: container.resolve(ExecutionScope)` with a container, `scope` of the platform without one). A cached read meant for every tenant says `shared: true`. The cache of tenants already in place is read cold once.
