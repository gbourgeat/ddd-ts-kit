**The endpoint of the audit trail starts with the endpoint.** `search-audit-entries.controller.ts` and `search-audit-entries.route.ts` held, before the endpoint, a hundred lines of schema: the query string, the view of an entry written four times (its interface, the required fields, the properties, the mapping), and the same lines in both variants. What the endpoint receives and answers now lives next to it, in files named after it: `audit-entries-parameters.ts` (the query string and the page limits), `audit-entries-filter-of.ts` (the filter of the query, from what a reader wrote), `audit-entry-view.ts` (the view and its schema, which says why its check is partial), `audit-entry-view-of.ts` (an entry as the endpoint answers it) and `audit-outcomes.ts` (the outcomes, held to the `AuditOutcome` of the kit). The controller and the route are thirty lines each and read as the endpoint; the example, which adds the network of practices to the filter and the impersonator to the view, follows.

To move: nothing; a project that keeps its endpoint in one file behaves the same.
