**With the identity preset, tenants and an audit trail, a command that succeeded on an organisation is audited in the chain of that organisation.** `auditMiddleware` of `@ddd-ts-kit/audit` takes `tenantOf(message)`: the entry of a message that succeeded is written with the tenant it names, in its chain, its `actor.tenantId` still the one of the token; a failure, and the decision of `auditDecisions`, stay in the tenant of the work, so a caller who names a tenant it cannot act in never writes to its chain nor opens one. The preset writes `platform/audit/organisation-of-command.ts`, which names the organisation of `OpenOrganisationCommand` (its slug) and of the six commands that carry `organisationId`, and gives it to the audit middleware of the commands in `platform/audit/create-audit.ts`: the owner of an organisation reads who opened it, invited, removed, changed a role or lent a right in it, whatever organisation the token of that person named. Accepting or declining an invitation names its token only and stays in the tenant of the token; the organisation joined reads `identity.member.joined` in its own chain, as before.

To move: in a project generated with the identity preset and tenants, write `src/platform/audit/organisation-of-command.ts` as `init --preset identity` now writes it, import it in `src/platform/audit/create-audit.ts` and add `tenantOf: organisationOfCommand` to the options of `auditMiddleware` of `commands`.
