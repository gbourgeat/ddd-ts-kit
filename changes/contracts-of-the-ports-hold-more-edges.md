**The contracts of `@ddd-ts-kit/testing` hold more of what an adapter answers at the edges.** `outboxStoreContract` takes an empty batch, an empty publication and an empty revival as nothing to do. The job store contract expects `false` for the heartbeat of a job the store does not hold. The event store contract loads a consignment again after a refused append and reads the winner's weight. A project that runs these contracts on an adapter of its own may see a new case fail: the case says what the port promises.

`auditTrailContract` now fails on its verification assertion, instead of the message "The second entry of the chain was not listed", when a storage does not list that entry. `controllerRoutes` of `@ddd-ts-kit/http` types the result of a tuple of one controller as a tuple of one route.
