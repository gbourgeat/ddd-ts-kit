**`init --force` keeps what the generators wired: at the same version `git diff` is empty.** It rewrote the files `init` owns from templates whose regions are empty, so the tables of a context left the schema index (on Drizzle the next `db:generate` wrote `DROP TABLE`), its events the audit trail, and the grants, the job settings, the values of the gateways and the policies went with them. Each rewritten file now takes the template of the new version with what its `ddd-ts-kit:*` regions held; the grants of `roles.ts`, the role vocabulary, the schemas and preview features of the Prisma datasource and the services of `docker-compose.yml` stay too, with the imports they name. The tables and the audited events a region does not hold, as after an earlier `--force`, are listed again from the tree, what a preset changed in the authorizer and the authenticator is applied again, and the journal of drizzle-kit is no longer emptied. A region the new version no longer holds is named in a note, file by file, with the command that writes its lines again.
