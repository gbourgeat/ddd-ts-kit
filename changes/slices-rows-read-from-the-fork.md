**`ddd-ts-kit merge-trunk` keeps a row of `docs/architecture/slices.md` the branch added before its squash when the trunk took the code without it, and `verify-merge` reports one that is lost.** The base of a branch that landed is found without the slices file, so it may hold a row, or a `done`, the trunk never took: merged from that base, the row read as deleted by the trunk and went, with `lost lines: none lost`. `verify-merge` now reads the row where the two sides parted before it calls a removal or a reopening the decision of the side brought in: a row the trunk never held is reported under `rows gone`, a status it never had under `rows no longer done`. When the base of `merge-trunk` is not that fork and holds another slices file, the rows are merged from the fork, shown under `automatic:`.
