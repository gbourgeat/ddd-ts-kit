**`ddd-ts-kit worker-scope --resume` and `status --workers` no longer show a new row as committed, `(by order)`, by the commit of a row that already landed.** The rows of a worker were read from its base by name. On a branch kept for its next rows, the commits of the rows the trunk took as one squash commit are still ahead of that name, and a commit without a `Slice:` trailer among them took the first new row. The rows are now read from the commit `worker-scope` recorded with the base (`baseCommit`) while the branch holds it, and only from the commits made on top of it.
