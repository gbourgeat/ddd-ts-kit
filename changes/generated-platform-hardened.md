**A body the HTTP framework refuses is answered as a problem and logged without the body.** `@ddd-ts-kit/http/express`, `@ddd-ts-kit/http/fastify` and `@ddd-ts-kit/http/hono` export `problemErrorHandler({ logger })`, and `@ddd-ts-kit/http/nest` exports `ProblemExceptionFilter`: what reaches the framework before a route of the kit is answered `application/problem+json` with the identifiers of the request, a body that is not JSON 400 `MALFORMED_BODY` (400 `BAD_REQUEST` on Nest, which turns it into an exception of its own first), a body over the limit of the parser 413 `BODY_TOO_LARGE`, another refusal of the framework with its own status (415 `UNSUPPORTED_MEDIA_TYPE` on Fastify, 404 `NOT_FOUND` on Nest), and a middleware or a hook that fails, the limit of the callers included, 500 `INTERNAL_ERROR` with no message. The logger receives the type of the error (`entity.parse.failed`), the status and the request id, never the message nor the body. Before, Express answered an HTML page, with the stack outside production, and wrote the error and the body it quotes to stderr; Fastify and Nest answered the message of the error. The generated `start-http-server.ts` installs it on the four frameworks, and the limit of the callers on Nest passes a failure on instead of leaving it unhandled. The problems of the Hono adapter carry `x-request-id` and the media type `application/problem+json; charset=utf-8`, as those of the pipeline, and title 413 `Content Too Large`.
