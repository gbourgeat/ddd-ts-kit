**The CLI prints less for an agent to read: `<command> --help` prints that command alone, `generate <kind> --help` that kind, and `--json` prints on one line.** Any `--help` printed the whole reference, about 41 KB, and `generate <kind> --help --json` every kind, about 27 KB. `<command> --help` (or `help <command>`) now prints the usage lines of the command, its note and the notes of the flags it takes (`describe --help`, 0.3 KB); `generate --help` adds the list of the kinds. `generate <kind> --help` prints the kind alone: its usage line, its summary, what it writes outside its context and when, whether it takes `--module`, and the flags every kind takes; with `--json` the same kind as one object, the entry `generate --help --json` lists for it. `ddd-ts-kit --help` and `generate --help --json` keep the whole reference. An option a command does not know is refused with the usage line of that command, or of the kind under `generate`, as its fix, instead of "Run `ddd-ts-kit --help`".

What a command prints under `--json` (and `check`, `affected` and `measures` under `--format json`) carried two spaces of indentation per level, a third of its size: it is now printed on one line, `describe <context> --json` on the scheduling example going from 45.8 KB to 32.5 KB and `list --json` from 25.3 KB to 15.4 KB. The keys are unchanged, and the JSON files the CLI writes (the config, `package.json`, the settings, the locks) keep the layout of the formatter.
