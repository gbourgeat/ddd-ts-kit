**An invitation of the identity preset holds while its issuer may still invite in its role.** `AcceptInvitationHandler` reads where the account that issued the invitation stands when the invitation is answered: removed from the organisation, deactivated, demoted below the role of the invitation, or no longer lent the right to invite by a delegation, its pending invitations are withdrawn and let nobody in (`INVITATION_WITHDRAWN`, a new refusal of the context, and the term `Withdrawn` of its glossary). `RoleRightsService.mayStillIssue` decides it, from the same table of rights as every other use case of the context.

To move: a project bootstrapped with `--preset identity` before this version keeps its handler. To hold the same rule, give `AcceptInvitationHandler` the `DelegationRepository`, and before saving the accepted invitation refuse it when `new RoleRightsService().mayStillIssue(invitation, { member, lent, now })` is false, `member` and `lent` read for `invitation.invitedBy` in its organisation (`findOf`, `findUnendedTo`).
