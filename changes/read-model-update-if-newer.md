**Breaking: `ReadModelStore.updateIfNewer` writes the change of a row another event created, and answers `missing` when that row is not there.** A projection wrote such an event (an appointment moved, a payment captured) as an `upsertIfNewer` whose `next` answered `undefined` when the key held no row, which read as `ignored`: nothing told a change that found no row from an event the read model filters. `updateIfNewer(key, stamp, change)` of `@ddd-ts-kit/kernel`, where `change` takes the row and answers the row, is the write of a partial change: `applied` on a row the key holds, `stale` when the row, or the trace of its deletion, holds this event or a later one, and `missing`, a fifth value of `ReadModelWrite`, when the key holds no row, the trace of a deletion older than the event, or a row of another tenant. Nothing is written then and the store never throws for it: the projection decides what a missing row means. It is not a cure for disorder: the store refuses to go back in time and cannot put back a change that arrived before its creation, or the older of two changes delivered out of order, which is `stale`. A partial change is right only on a feed that keeps the order of a key; an event that carries the whole row is right in any order. `docs/concepts/cqrs.md`, "Which write for which event", says which feeds keep that order (the save, a catch-up subscription, the in-process bus, the relay per key, Kafka per partition) and what to do with `missing` on each. `projection_events_total` of `@ddd-ts-kit/di` counts the result `missing`. `TableReadModelStore`, so the stores of `@ddd-ts-kit/knex`, `@ddd-ts-kit/drizzle`, `@ddd-ts-kit/prisma`, `@ddd-ts-kit/typeorm` and `@ddd-ts-kit/opensearch` and `InMemoryReadModelStore`, and `ShadowReadModelStore` have it; `readModelStoreContract`, `readModelRowsContract` and `readModelContract` of `@ddd-ts-kit/testing` hold the cases; `generate projection` of `ddd-ts-kit` names the two writes in the method it leaves to fill. `upsertIfNewer` keeps its meaning.

To move: a class of your own that extends `ReadModelStore` without extending `TableReadModelStore` implements `updateIfNewer`. A `switch` over `ReadModelWrite` gains the case `missing`, and a dashboard that sums the results of `projection_events_total` meets it. In a projection, `upsertIfNewer(key, stamp, (row) => (row === undefined ? undefined : { ...row, ... }))` may become `updateIfNewer(key, stamp, (row) => ({ ...row, ... }))`: its answer is then `missing` where it was `ignored`. Never throw on `missing` in a projection fed by the domain events of its context or by the event store; prefer events that carry the whole row where the transport keeps no order.
