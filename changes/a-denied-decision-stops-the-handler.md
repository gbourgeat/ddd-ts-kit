**A handler that goes on with a success after `Access` denied its permission is refused, at run time and by `authz/policy-asked`.** When a policy refines the permission of a use case, the authorization module now refuses a handler that answers with a success (an `ok` Result, or a value that is no Result) when its last question on that permission was not allowed, with `PolicyDenialIgnoredError` (exported by `@ddd-ts-kit/auth`), inside the unit of work, so that its change is rolled back. A handler that stops on the denial with a failed Result (`err(...)`) or a throw answers its own refusal, unchanged; a denial followed by an allowed question on another element passes. `authz/policy-asked` reports a `decide` or `decideOn` on that permission written as a statement of its own, its decision dropped, with a fix that names `authorize`.

To move: a handler that goes on with a success after a denial of `access.decide(...)` calls `access.authorize(...)`, which refuses a denial, or keeps the decision and stops when `decision.allowed` is false, with a failed Result or a throw.
