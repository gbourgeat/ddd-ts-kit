**A handler that asks `Access` and goes on after a denial is refused, at run time and by `authz/policy-asked`.** When a policy refines the permission of a use case, the authorization module now refuses the result of a handler whose last question on that permission was not allowed, with `PolicyDenialIgnoredError` (exported by `@ddd-ts-kit/auth`), inside the unit of work, so that its change is rolled back; a denial followed by an allowed question on another element passes. `authz/policy-asked` reports a `decide` or `decideOn` on that permission written as a statement of its own, its decision dropped, with a fix that names `authorize`.

To move: a handler that calls `access.decide(...)` and ignores the answer calls `access.authorize(...)`, which refuses a denial, or keeps the decision and stops when `decision.allowed` is false.
