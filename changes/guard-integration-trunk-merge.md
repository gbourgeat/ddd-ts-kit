**A worker that merges an integration branch is no longer stopped by the guard of the plugin.** The guard took what a merge brought in for the trunk's only when the other side was on the branch `origin/HEAD` names, `main` or `master`: with workers cut from an integration branch (`worker-scope --base work/<plan>`), every file of that branch outside the scope of the worker was held as its own change after `merge-trunk`, each command and write was refused, and the refusal named `git checkout <commit> -- <path>` and `git rm`, which would have undone the trunk in the branch. The `base` that `.ddd-ts-kit/worker.json` records is now a trunk too, ahead of the three others, during the work and at the stop of the worker. A merge of a branch that is no trunk is still held, and the refusal then names no command: the worker undoes nothing and reports the merge to the conductor. The refusal of a file changed outside the scope reads "Your commands, or another session, changed files outside your scope", since the guard sees the change, not who made it.
