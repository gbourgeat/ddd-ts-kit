**`init --authn none`: the development header is believed where `NODE_ENV` says `development` or `test`, never by default.** `loadConfig` refuses to start a project on the development header when `NODE_ENV` is left out or empty (`NODE_ENV must be development or test`), and `DevelopmentHeaderAuthenticator` is built in `development` and `test` only, where it refused `production` alone. The Dockerfile sets `production`, the end-to-end specs `test`, `.env.example` `development`, as before.

To move: a project on `--authn none` started without `NODE_ENV` sets it, `NODE_ENV=development` in its `.env` (the line of `.env.example`). A project generated before keeps its files; to hold the same rule, add the check after the line that reads `NODE_ENV` in `src/platform/config/load-config.ts` (`if (env.optional('NODE_ENV') === undefined) env.problem(...)`), and refuse in the constructor of `DevelopmentHeaderAuthenticator` every environment but `development` and `test`.
