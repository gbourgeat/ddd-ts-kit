**`generate aggregate <context> <Name> --birth "<Fact>"` names the fact an aggregate is born with, writes its domain event, and no template invents `registeredAt` or `bornAt` any more.** The fact is written in business words, `"Invoice Issued"`, `InvoiceIssued` or `Issued` naming the same `InvoiceIssued` (`billing.invoice.issued`), judged by the glossary like any name and refused when it does not read as something that happened. The command writes the event and its spec as `generate domain-event` does; `create(id, now)` records it at the instant the handler takes from its `Clock`; the builder takes that instant named after the fact (`anInvoice().issuedAt(at)`) and the spec proves the birth (`records the fact of its birth, invoice issued, at the instant it is given`). Without the flag, the aggregate keeps no instant of its own, and the `TODO(domain)` of `create` says how to record the birth later without rewriting anything: `generate domain-event`, one `record` line, then `generate builder`. The repository no longer writes a `registered_at` column, field or mapping on any ORM: the audit column `created_at` dates the row, and an instant a rule reads is a field of the state named after its fact, with its column. `generate builder` reads the event `create` records, whatever it is given, replays it for an event-sourced aggregate, names in a note the parameters of `create` it cannot guess, and writes no builder, saying why, when `create` is missing or records an event not named `<Aggregate><Fact>`. The event-sourced path takes the same flag as before (`--birth Triggered`, now also `--birth "Incident Triggered"`), its state, builder and spec name the instant after the fact (`triggeredAt`, `createdAt` without the flag). `--retention created_at:<period>` is refused as `--retention registered_at:<period>` still is, for the tables generated before.

To move: an aggregate generated before keeps its `registeredAt` and its `registered_at` column. A new aggregate has neither: generate it with `--birth "<Fact>"` (`generate aggregate billing Invoice --birth "Invoice Issued"`) to get its birth event and the instant named after it, and a repository generated for it writes no `registered_at`; a team that read `registeredAt` on a new aggregate reads the `occurredAt` of its birth event, or adds the field and its column by hand. On an event-sourced aggregate generated anew, `registeredAt` is now `createdAt` (or `<fact>At` with `--birth`), and the builder method `bornAt(...)` is `createdAt(...)`.
